## Q: How do you determine if a Yang Metal (庚) Day Master born in Snake month (巳) is strong or weak?

A: A Yang Metal (庚) Day Master born in Snake month (巳, May) is born in early summer. Although Snake month contains Hidden Stem Yang Metal (庚) as a secondary element (weight 0.1), the primary element of Snake is Yang Fire (丙, weight 0.6), which controls and melts Metal. Therefore, without strong Earth (戊/己) in the stem or branch to act as a bridge (Fire -> Earth -> Metal), the Day Master is generally classified as **Weak (身弱)**. The primary Useful God (用神) needed is Wet Earth (己/辰/丑) to absorb Fire and generate Metal, or Water (壬/癸) to cool the summer heat.

## Q: What is the effect of True Solar Time on hour pillar determination for Bangkok (100.49°E)?

A: Standard Thailand Time (UTC+7) uses the meridian at 105.0°E. Bangkok is located at 100.49°E, creating a longitude offset of -18.04 minutes (since Bangkok is 4.51° west of the standard meridian). Adding the Equation of Time (EoT), which varies up to ±16 minutes throughout the year, the total True Solar Time (TST) difference can shift the birth time by 20–35 minutes relative to standard clock time. For births near two-hour branch boundaries (such as 12:50 PM or 2:55 PM), TST correction is essential to determine whether the birth fell in Horse (午) or Goat (未) hour pillar.
