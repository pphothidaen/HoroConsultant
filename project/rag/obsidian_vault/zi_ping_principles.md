# Zi Ping Zhen Quan (子平真詮) — Core Principles

## Day Master Strength and Seasonality

The strength of the Day Master (日主) depends primarily on the Month Branch (月令).
When the Day Master is born in a season that supports its element, it is considered in season (得時).

- **Wood** is strong in Spring (寅, 卯) and nourished by Water in Winter (亥, 子).
- **Fire** is strong in Summer (巳, 午) and nourished by Wood in Spring.
- **Metal** is strong in Autumn (申, 酉) and nourished by Earth in Late Seasons (辰, 戌, 丑, 未).
- **Water** is strong in Winter (亥, 子) and nourished by Metal in Autumn.
- **Earth** is strong in the four seasonal transitions (辰, 戌, 丑, 未) and supported by Fire.

## Useful God (用神) Selection Rules

1. **Weak Day Master**: Needs Resource (印星) to generate or Companion (比劫) to support.
2. **Strong Day Master**: Needs Output (食傷) to exhaust, Wealth (財星) to drain, or Officer/Killings (官殺) to control.
3. **Extreme Heat or Cold**: Requires Useful God of Regulation (調候用神).
   - Winter births (亥, 子, 丑) require Fire (丙, 丁) for warmth.
   - Summer births (巳, 午, 未) require Water (壬, 癸) for cooling.
